10-Month Film Production Program

Click each stage to read full details. Learn by doing.

Duration: 2 Months | Foundation Stage

What is Pre-Production?

This is where your film is born on paper. No cameras yet. We plan everything so you don’t waste money on set. 80% of filmmaking problems are solved here.

What You’ll Learn:

  • Story Development: Turn Gulu experiences into film ideas. 3-Act Structure.
  • Screenplay Formatting: Industry standard using free Celtx software.
  • Storyboarding: Draw your film like a comic book. Plan every shot.
  • Budgeting: How to make a 5M UGX film look like 50M.

Final Project:

Complete 8-10 page short film script + pitch deck ready for investors.

Duration: 4 Months | Shooting Stage

What is Production?

This is the shoot. You + crew + actors + camera = movie. We train you on real gear at Gulu Creative Hub. You’ll be holding cameras from Week 1.

What You’ll Learn:

  • Directing: Working with actors, visual storytelling, running a set.
  • Cinematography: Camera operation, lenses, composition, gimbal movement.
  • Lighting & Sound: 3-Point lighting with LEDs, natural light hacks, boom mic recording.
  • Live Shoot: Direct your 5-minute short film in Gulu with classmates as crew.

Gear You’ll Use:

Canon M50, Sony a6400, Godox LED lights, Rode mics, DJI gimbal - all provided.

Students filming on location in Gulu

Duration: 4 Months | Editing Stage

What is Post-Production?

This is where bad footage becomes a good film, and good footage becomes great. 50% of film is sound.

What You’ll Learn:

  • Adobe Premiere Pro: Cutting on action, J-cuts, L-cuts, pacing.
  • Color Grading: Fix bad lighting + create mood in DaVinci Resolve.
  • Sound Design: Adding room tone, footsteps, music. Export for YouTube/Cinema.
  • VFX Basics: Remove objects, green screen, text animations.

Final Project:

Completed 5-minute short film with color, sound, music, credits. Festival-ready.

Duration: 1 Month | Release Stage

What is Distribution?

A film no one sees is a dead film. We teach you how to get your work on phones, TVs, and festivals — and get paid.

What You’ll Learn:

  • Film Festivals: Submit to Uganda Film Festival, Zanzibar Film Fest, Africa Magic.
  • YouTube Strategy: Thumbnails, titles, SEO so Gulu audiences find your film.
  • Monetization: YouTube ads, client work, wedding/music video gigs.
  • Portfolio Building: Create your own website + showreel to attract paid jobs.

Final Project:

Your film uploaded with 1000+ views + strategy to land your first paid gig.
